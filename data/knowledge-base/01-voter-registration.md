# Voter Registration

## Who can register

To be enrolled as a voter in India, a person must:

- Be a citizen of India
- Be at least 18 years old as of 1 January, 1 April, 1 July, or 1 October of the year of revision (the four qualifying dates introduced by ECI in 2022)
- Be ordinarily resident in the constituency for which they want to register

A person can be registered as a voter in only one constituency. Registering in two places is an offence under Section 17 of the Representation of the People Act, 1950.

## How to register

There are three ways to apply:

1. **Online via the National Voter Service Portal (NVSP)** at https://voters.eci.gov.in/. Fill Form 6 (for first-time voters) or Form 8 (for shifting/correction). Upload a passport-size photograph, age proof, and address proof.
2. **Through the Voter Helpline mobile app**, available on Android and iOS. Same forms, easier on mobile.
3. **In person** by submitting Form 6 to your local Booth Level Officer (BLO) or Electoral Registration Officer (ERO).

## Documents required

- Recent passport-size colour photograph
- One proof of age (Aadhaar, birth certificate, 10th class marksheet, passport, PAN, or driving licence)
- One proof of address (Aadhaar, passport, utility bill, bank passbook, rental agreement, or ration card)

## What happens after submission

Your application is verified by the Booth Level Officer who may visit your residence. If approved, your name is added to the electoral roll and an Elector Photo Identity Card (EPIC) is sent to your registered address by post within 30 to 40 days. You can also download a digital EPIC (e-EPIC) from NVSP.

## Special categories

- **Service voters** (military, paramilitary, government posted abroad) register through Form 2/2A and may vote by postal ballot
- **NRIs** (Non-Resident Indians) can register using Form 6A and currently must vote in person at their home constituency
- **Persons with disabilities** can request priority assistance and accessible polling booths through the Saksham app
