# Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) and VVPAT

## What is an EVM

An Electronic Voting Machine is a stand-alone device used to record and count votes in Indian elections. It has two units connected by a 5-metre cable:

- **Ballot Unit (BU)**: kept inside the voting compartment with candidate names, party symbols, and a button next to each
- **Control Unit (CU)**: kept with the Presiding Officer; it activates the BU after the voter's identity is verified

EVMs are not connected to any network — they are completely offline machines, which makes them immune to remote hacking. Each EVM can record up to 2000 votes and accommodate 64 candidates (16 per ballot unit, expandable up to 4 units).

## What is VVPAT

The Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail is a printer-like device attached to the EVM, introduced in 2013. When a voter casts their vote, the VVPAT prints a paper slip showing:

- The serial number of the candidate
- The candidate's name
- The party symbol

The slip is visible to the voter through a transparent window for 7 seconds, then drops into a sealed box. The box is stored with the Returning Officer and used for verification during counting.

## VVPAT verification during counting

After EVM votes are counted, VVPAT slips from 5 randomly selected polling booths per assembly segment are physically counted and matched against the EVM totals. If there is any discrepancy, the VVPAT count prevails. This sample size was increased from 1 booth to 5 by the Supreme Court in 2019.

## Mock polls

Before voting begins, a mock poll is conducted at every booth in the presence of polling agents. At least 50 votes are cast on the EVM, the VVPAT slips are matched, and the EVM is then cleared and sealed for actual polling. This proves to all parties that the machine is functioning correctly.

## EVM security and storage

EVMs are stored in strong rooms under 24-hour CCTV monitoring and armed guard from the time of polling until counting. Every party can post observers at the strong room. The seals on the EVM are checked by candidates' agents before counting begins.
