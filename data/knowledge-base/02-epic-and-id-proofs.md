# EPIC Card and Accepted Identity Documents

## What is the EPIC

The Elector Photo Identity Card (EPIC) is the official voter ID card issued by the Election Commission of India. It contains the voter's photograph, name, age, address, EPIC number (a unique 10-character alphanumeric ID), and the constituency name.

The EPIC was introduced in 1993 to reduce voter impersonation. It is widely accepted as a proof of identity beyond elections, including for opening bank accounts and government services.

## Digital EPIC (e-EPIC)

Since January 2021, voters can download a non-editable PDF version of their EPIC from NVSP or the Voter Helpline app. The e-EPIC is a valid identity proof at the polling booth. New voters get a notification to download it once their registration is approved.

## Voting without an EPIC

If you do not have your EPIC on polling day, the Election Commission accepts 11 alternative photo identity documents:

1. Aadhaar card
2. PAN card
3. Passport
4. Driving licence
5. Service identity card issued by Central or State Government, PSUs, or public limited companies (with photo)
6. Passbook with photo, issued by a bank or post office
7. Smart card issued by RGI under NPR
8. MNREGA job card
9. Health insurance smart card under Ministry of Labour scheme
10. Pension document with photograph
11. Official identity card issued to MPs, MLAs, and MLCs

Your name must already be in the electoral roll — alternative IDs do not let you vote if you are unregistered.

## What if my EPIC has wrong details

Use Form 8 on NVSP to correct your name spelling, address, photograph, or other details. Corrections are processed within a few weeks and a fresh EPIC is reissued.
